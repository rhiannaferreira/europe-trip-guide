# About the Tripadvisor Terra Platform

Welcome to the Terra Platform - Tripadvisor's comprehensive content delivery platform for partners.

Terra (<Anchor target="_blank" href="https://www.tripadvisor.com/developers"><https://tripadvisor.com/developers></Anchor>) is a powerful way to bring Tripadvisor's trusted travel content, advice, and information to your platform. Our endpoints and data feeds are structured around how travelers experience and engage with Tripadvisor. While primarily an API, Terra supports delivery through secure data feeds as well.

* **Locations** - rich content from the world's most traveled points-of-interest (POIs), accommodations, and restaurants.
* **Reviews** - the unique and authentic opinions and ratings our travelers share about the places they visit
* **Photos** - media content and visual assets related to those locations and reviews
* **Geos** - information about the destinations around the world: from countries down to specific neighborhoods

In addition to our content, Terra offers services that also expose our frontier AI capabilities for partners, such as Agentic Search, an endpoint that recommends top locations optimized for agentic use cases.

### What Do I Get Access To?

Terra is distributed based on tiered packages with greater access and usage rights in higher tiers. Access is determined in two ways: content access and platform access. Discover, for example, gets you our most common, factual Location content, and a limited number of UGC (User Generated Content) such as Reviews and Photos. It is also only available through API access with lower throughput and quotas.

As your needs and use cases grow, subsequent packages will allow you to scale your usage and access. Get in touch with our Business Development Team if you have questions or explore your dashboard for more information.
